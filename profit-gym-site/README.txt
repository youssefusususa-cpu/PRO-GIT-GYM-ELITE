Upload to Google AI Studio:

3. Upload/extract this folder or provide index.html and the assets folder to the project.
4. Ask Gemini to preserve the current design and images, then improve or convert the project as needed.
5. Verify phone, social links, address, opening hours, membership prices, and map pin before publishing.

The site intentionally does not invent membership prices, class schedules, reviews, or exact GPS coordinates.
